# Contributing

By submitting a contribution, you retain your copyright and agree to provide
prose, diagrams, and knowledge metadata under CC BY-SA 4.0 and repository-local
source code under MPL 2.0. No copyright assignment is required. Shared engine
code belongs in `noetheca/knowledge-engine`.

Do not submit copied text, images, exercises, or diagrams without verified
compatible permission. Record third-party source, author, license or permission,
and modifications in `ATTRIBUTION.md`. AI-assisted drafts require human review
of facts, sources, relationships, and licensing.
